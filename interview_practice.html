About practice modes and external tools
Choose your practice mode: Free guided practice uses built-in questions and answer-pattern tips, with no API or payment. AI interviewer mode sends your interview answers to OpenAI and needs an API key with prepaid API billing. Each AI practice is limited to 10 answers. It is not active until you select that mode and press Start. Ask your teacher before setting up billing. Typing, microphone recognition, and voice playback depend on your browser.
